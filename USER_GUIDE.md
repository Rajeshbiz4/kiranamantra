# KiranaMitra 1.0.0 Quick User Guide

1. Complete Store Profile and create staff users.
2. Add suppliers, customers and products.
3. Record purchases to increase inventory and capture batch/expiry details.
4. Use New Invoice to scan products, take payment and print a bill.
5. Use Returns for credit notes and debit notes.
6. Record customer collections and supplier payments in Ledgers.
7. Record daily expenses and close the Cash Register.
8. Review low stock, expired batches and business reports.
9. Create a manual database backup before major changes.

Roles: Owner has full access, Manager cannot access sensitive administration pages, and Cashier has billing-focused access.

Product CSV import columns:

```text
name,code,barcode,category,purchasePrice,sellingPrice,gstRate,stock,reorderLevel
```
